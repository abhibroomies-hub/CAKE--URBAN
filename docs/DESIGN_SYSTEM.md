# Design System Specification — Cake Urban

## 1. Color System
- **Primary Luxury Accent**: Gold / Champagne `#DFB15B`
- **Secondary Accent**: Rose Gold `#DE9088`
- **Background Neutral (Light)**: High-contrast Off-White `#FAFAFA` with border accents `#F1F5F9`
- **Background Luxury (Dark)**: Velvet Obsidian `#140603` / Deep Charcoal `#1A1A1A`
- **Text Primary**: Charcoal Obsidian `#0F172A`
- **Text Secondary**: Muted Slate `#64748B`

## 2. Typography
- **Headings & Display**: Playfair Display / Plus Jakarta Sans Bold
- **Body & Controls**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter Variable
- **Labels & Buttons**: Single-line, non-wrapping `white-space: nowrap` with responsive padding scaling.

## 3. 3D & Elevation Standards
- **3D Interactive Tilt**:
  - `perspective`: 1000px
  - `maxTilt`: 12° to 18°
  - `scaleHover`: 1.03x to 1.04x
  - `glareOpacity`: 0.15 to 0.3
- **Borders & Shadows**: `rounded-[20px]` to `rounded-[32px]` with soft layered shadows `shadow-[0_25px_80px_rgba(0,0,0,0.08)]`.
